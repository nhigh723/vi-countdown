## Description (4000 chars max)

```
How long until the biggest game launch of 2026? Countdown VI tells you —
down to the second.

LAUNCH COUNTDOWN
A big, live countdown to launch day: Thursday, November 19, 2026. Days,
hours, minutes, seconds — ticking in real time.

WHEN CAN I PLAY?
Pick your console — PlayStation 5 or Xbox Series X|S — and see your local
unlock time, pre-load status, and what to have ready: clear at least 212 GB
of space, and know that the physical edition is a code in a box (no disc).

LAUNCH TIMELINE
The three dates that matter: digital pre-loading from November 10, the
physical edition at retailers from November 12, and launch day, November 19.

GET READY FOR LAUNCH
A checklist of seven things to sort before midnight on launch night: clear
212 GB of space, pre-load the game, confirm your edition, and more. Tick
them off as you go — your progress is saved on your device.

LAUNCH-NIGHT REMINDER
Flip one switch and get a notification 5 minutes before launch — Wednesday,
November 18 at 11:55 PM, your local time. No account, nothing leaves your
device.

WHO PLAYS FIRST?
Midnight local everywhere — see when each city's midnight lands in your
time, from Auckland first to Los Angeles last.

SHARE YOUR COUNTDOWN
One tap turns the countdown into a shareable card — your days and hours to
go, ready for Messages, socials, or anywhere.

WHAT WE KNOW
The story, the setting, and the cast — everything Rockstar has officially
confirmed, with no rumors and no leaks. Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos, the
state of Leonida and its six named regions, and the full cast Rockstar has
profiled.

THE GAME, IN BRIEF
Editions and prices (Standard $79.99, Ultimate $99.99), platforms, and the
public facts — every line traced to an official source listed in the app.

OFFICIAL NEWS
One-tap links to the official sources: Rockstar Newswire, the official game
page, and the PlayStation and Microsoft stores. Links only — no copied
articles, no rumors.

AFTER LAUNCH
When the countdown hits zero, the app flips to launch mode — and the 100%
completion checklist (missions, collectibles) is coming in the next update.

Free. No ads. No account. No data collected. Works offline.

---
Unofficial fan app. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to
Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. All visuals are original.
```
