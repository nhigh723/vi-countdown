Privacy Policy — Countdown VI

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Countdown VI is an unofficial fan companion app that counts down to a video game launch. This policy explains what data the app collects — in short: none.

Data we collect

We collect no personal data. The app has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising SDK, and no tracking of any kind. It does not access your photos, contacts, location, or any other device data, and it sends nothing to any server.

Data stored on your device

The app stores two small settings on your device only: which console (PlayStation 5 or Xbox Series X|S) you picked in the unlock checker, so it remembers your choice, and which items you have ticked off in the "Get ready for launch" checklist, so your progress is saved. Those settings never leave your device, and deleting the app deletes them.

Links to other sites

The app contains links to official public websites (Rockstar Games, PlayStation, Microsoft/Xbox). Tapping a link opens it in your browser; those sites have their own privacy policies, and this policy does not cover them.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here and the "last updated" date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer through the App Store listing for Countdown VI.

Countdown VI is an unofficial fan app. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.
