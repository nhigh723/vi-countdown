Privacy Policy — Countdown VI

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Countdown VI is an unofficial fan companion app that counts down to a video game launch. This policy explains what data the app collects — in short: the app itself collects none. The only data involved anywhere is what Apple and RevenueCat need to process the optional one-time Launch Pass purchase (see "In-app purchases" below).

Data we collect

We collect no personal data. The app has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising SDK, and no tracking of any kind. It does not access your photos, contacts, location, or any other device data. Outside of the optional Launch Pass purchase described below, it sends nothing to any server.

Data stored on your device

The app stores three small settings on your device only: which console (PlayStation 5 or Xbox Series X|S) you picked in the unlock checker, so it remembers your choice; which items you have ticked off in the "Get ready for launch" checklist, so your progress is saved; and whether you turned the launch-night reminder on, so it stays set. Those settings never leave your device, and deleting the app deletes them. Sharing the countdown card also writes a temporary image of the countdown to the device's cache so it can be shared; it contains no personal data, and the system clears it when the cache is cleaned.

If you buy the Launch Pass, the app also keeps a small on-device note that you own it, so your Founder badge shows even when you are offline. That note is only a mirror of your purchase record held by Apple and RevenueCat — the app re-checks it with RevenueCat when it opens, and deleting the app deletes it.

In-app purchases (Launch Pass)

The Launch Pass is a one-time $1.99 purchase, sold through the App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple — the app never sees your card details. To know whether you own the pass (so it can show your Founder badge and your ad-free checklist), the app checks your purchase status through RevenueCat, the purchase platform. RevenueCat receives only what Apple provides for that purpose: your purchase record and the device identifiers Apple attaches to it. It does not receive your countdown, your checklist, or any other data from the app. If you tap "Restore Purchases", the same check runs so your pass can be restored on another device signed in with the same Apple ID. RevenueCat's own privacy policy governs what it does with that data: revenuecat.com/privacy.

Links to other sites

The app contains links to official public websites (Rockstar Games, PlayStation, Microsoft/Xbox). Tapping a link opens it in your browser; those sites have their own privacy policies, and this policy does not cover them.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children. Purchases are processed by Apple under Apple's policies.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here and the "last updated" date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer through the App Store listing for Countdown VI.
